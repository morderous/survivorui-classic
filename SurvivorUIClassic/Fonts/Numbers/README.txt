Fonts for the game's own damage numbers (the options' General tab, Combat text).

Scaled copies, at 90% down to 30% by tens, of Barlow Condensed SemiBold in the folder above,
subset to Latin letters, their hinting and kerning left out, renamed "SUIC Numbers Barlow <size>".

Each stays under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, as its original (OFL-Barlow.txt).
